3. Value Proposition per Partner Type

For Integration Partners (AlayaCare — Primary Target)
For Integration Partners (AlayaCare — Primary Target)
"Integrating  Home-Care-AI  into  AlayaCare  means  care  coordinators  fill  every  vacant  visit  using
trust-weighted  matching  without  leaving  their  existing  scheduling  workflow.  Agencies  that  currently
use AlayaCare's Vacant Visit Agent — which matches on availability and qualifications only — get a
trust layer and a client notification chain that AlayaCare does not offer natively. The integration makes
Home-Care-AI the matching intelligence behind AlayaCare's scheduling engine, not a replacement for
it."
The  VI-1  unlock  this  creates:  Agency  owners  currently  resistant  to  "another  tool"  have  no  objection  when
Home-Care-AI surfaces within their existing AlayaCare workflow. VI-1 (standalone willingness to pay) becomes
less critical if the product lives inside the coordinator's current environment.
