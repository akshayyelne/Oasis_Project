1. Geoffrey Moore Positioning Statement
1. Geoffrey Moore Positioning Statement
Forhome care coordinators at independent Australian agencies managing 20–200 clients
wholose  30–60  minutes  and  cancel  1  in  5  visits  every  time  a  carer  calls  in  sick  —  because  no
system knows which replacement the client will actually open the door for
Home-Care-AIis a trust-weighted vacancy matching platform
thatfills every vacant visit in under 5 minutes with the right carer for that client, and automatically
notifies the carer, the client, and the family in the right order — so that no senior sits in their
chair waiting, and no family finds out before their parent does
UnlikeAlayaCare's Vacant Visit Agent, which matches on availability and qualifications only
our productmatches on who the client trusts — using a Soft Preference Profile built from the coordinator's
institutional knowledge — so the coordinator approves once, and the right person arrives.

<b>White  Space  we  own:</b>  High  trust-weighted  matching  +  coordinator-approved  agentic  workflow.  No
competitor operates in this quadrant. AlayaCare occupies the "automated but trust-blind" quadrant — fast, but it still
produces  the  7  calls  Angela  makes  after  AlayaCare  suggests  an  unfamiliar  carer.  Manual  calls  are  slow  and
trust-dependent but rely entirely on coordinator memory. Home-Care-AI is the only product that is both.
Competitive notes
•AlayaCare  is  the  primary  reference  competitor  —  not  because  it  is  the  closest  product  but  because  it  is  the
tool coordinators already compare against. Naming it anchors the differentiation.
•HCP  /  Brevity  /  Carelink+  are  not  named  in  the  primary  positioning  —  they  are  not  the  mental  model  our
beachhead uses.
•Manual calls + spreadsheet is the real competitive alternative — coordinators default to this, not AlayaCare,
when no better tool exists. Both alternatives must be addressed in messaging.
