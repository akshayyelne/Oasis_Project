Amendment Integrated: The Audit Trauma (Amendment A2)
Two years ago, Priya testified at a coroner's inquest. A patient at her previous agency (Gerald, 84) died from complications following a
missed insulin dose. Priya had provided the care correctly, but her 'summary-only' notes said 'Insulin administered as per care plan. Patient
stable.' The solicitor asked: what time? Her notes said 'morning visit.' What was the BGL? 'Within normal limits' — no number. What
injection site? Not recorded. The coroner found no neglect, but Priya was traumatised. She now carries a notebook, writing exact times,
readings, and words during visits, then transcribes to the EMR — doubling her documentation time. She tells David: 'If you can't point to a
timestamp and a number, you didn't do it.'

• AMENDMENT A2: Coroner's inquest (2 yrs ago) — couldn't prove care with summary-only notes. Surfaces #3 Voice-to-Docs + Article IX.

Design Implications
• Voice-to-docs must produce structured, timestamped, field-specific records — not summaries.
• Audit log non-nullable fields (Article IX) should be enforced at the input layer.
• This backstory makes the audit schema feel like protection for nurses, not surveillance.
Constitutional Alignment
Article IX — Audit Log Schema
Priya's experience justifies every non-nullable field: timestamp, confidence score, action taken, trigger value. Her testimony failed because
the system produced nullable, unstructured records.
Article VII — Audit Log = State of Truth
'If a state transition happened and was not logged, it did not happen.' Priya lived this rule. The hash-chain integrity isn't over-engineering —
it's 'never again.'

Design Implications
• Family-facing interfaces must show interpreted summaries, not raw data.
• Alert thresholds must be role-specific: clinicians see all flags, families see only high-confidence.

• The system should reduce nurse burden, not increase it via anxious-family data queries.
Constitutional Alignment
Capability #4 Design Requirement
Never present raw data to families. Always present clinically interpreted, confidence-calibrated status summaries. The chat answers 'Is mum

Capability #1 Alert Calibration
Family-facing confidence threshold must be HIGHER than clinical-facing. Clinicians see yellow. Families only see red.
