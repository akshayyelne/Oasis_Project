INTERVIEW FIELD KIT  |  Home-Care-AI  |  Carry into every session
OPENING (2 min) — Say this first
"Hi [Name], thanks for making time. We're researching how families and care professionals manage day-to-day care for seniors at home. We're not selling anything —
we're here to learn from your real experience. No right or wrong answers. Is it okay if I take notes / record? We have [30/45/60] minutes. Does that work?"
WARM-UP (5 min) — Context & Background
"Tell me about your role — what does a typical week look like for you?"
"How long have you been caring for / working with seniors at home?"
"How many people are you responsible for right now?"
(FC only) "Are you the primary contact, or are other family members involved?"
(HCN/CC) "What tools does your agency currently use to track patient status?"
BLOCK 1: FALLS
"Walk me through the LAST TIME someone you care for had a fall. What
happened from the very beginning?"
"What did you do first? Then what? Who else was involved?"
"How long did the whole response take?"
"What was the hardest part of that situation?"
"What info did you wish you had but didn't?"
"Was there a moment you weren't sure whether to call 911 or wait?"
"Have you ever gotten a false alarm? What happened?"
"What would have made that situation easier or safer?"
BLOCK 2: MEDICATIONS
"Tell me about the last time there was a problem with a medication —
missed dose, wrong dose, confusion."
"How did you find out? How long after it happened?"
"What did you do? Who else was looped in?"
"What's the most frustrating part of keeping track of medications?"
"What do you use today to manage this? How does it break down?"
"Have you been unsure whether a dose was taken or not? What did you
do?"
"If this worked perfectly, what would that look like day to day?"
BLOCK 3: COMMUNICATION & ESCALATION
"When something concerning happens, how does info get to you? Walk
me through the chain."
"What's the weakest link — where does info get lost or delayed?"
"Have you ever second-guessed a decision to escalate or not? Tell me
about that."
"How do you decide: emergency vs. watch-and-wait?"
(HCN/MD) "When you get a device alert, how much do you trust it?"
BLOCK 4: CURRENT TOOLS & WORKAROUNDS
"What tools/systems do you use to monitor or coordinate care?"
"Walk me through how you used [tool] last week."
"What does it do well? Where does it fall short?"
"Have you tried anything new in the last year? What happened?"
"What would you give up to have a system that actually worked?"
BLOCK 5: WILLINGNESS TO PRIORITIZE (Do NOT pitch — past behavior only)
"How much time per week do you spend on coordination and communication around care?"
"Does your agency/family currently pay for any monitoring technology? What?"
"Have you ever evaluated a care tool and decided NOT to buy? What made you say no?"
"If a tool solved [biggest pain], what would a fair price look like?"
When You Hear...Probe With...
Vague answer"Can you give me a specific example?"
Strong emotion"Tell me more about that feeling. What was at stake?"
Surface answer"Why?" (ask gently, up to 3x)
Story stops short"What happened next?"
Mentions someone"Who is that person? How did they fit in?"
Mentions workaround"How long have you done it that way?"
THE MOM TEST — ACTIVE REMINDERS
Ask about THEIR LIFE, not your idea
Ask about THE PAST — never "Would you use X?"
Talk less, listen more — target 80/20 split
NEVER PITCH during the interview
Compliments are noise — "Sounds useful!" = nothing
Strong emotions = REAL SIGNAL — dig in immediately
WRAP-UP (3 min)
"Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?" | "Who else should I talk to?" | "Can I follow up with you?" | Thank them. Promise to share findings.
