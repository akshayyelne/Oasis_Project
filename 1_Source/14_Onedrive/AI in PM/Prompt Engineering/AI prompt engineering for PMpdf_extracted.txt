Summary
What is Prompt Engineering?
In today’s rapidly evolving world of artificial intelligence and natural language 
processing, project managers are increasingly turning to generative AI systems 
powered by large language models (LLMs) like ChatGPT, Infinity, Gemini, and Claude 
to streamline workflows and enhance project outcomes. These models have the 
potential to revolutionize project management tasks, from generating documentation 
to analyzing data and providing insights. However, to fully harness the power of these 
models, project managers must master the art of prompt engineering.
Prompts are your input into generative AI systems to obtain specific results. Prompt 
engineering is a vital skill that involves the meticulous crafting, testing, and refining of 
prompts to guide generative AI models toward accurate, relevant, and useful outputs. 
This process is essential for optimizing workflows, boosting productivity, and driving 
project success. Effective prompt engineering requires several key strategies: crafting 
specific and structured prompts, providing relevant context, setting the appropriate 
tone, including examples, experimenting and refining prompts, understanding the 
audience, using clear language, incorporating structure, and adding reliability checks.

What is Prompt Engineering? (continued)
A fundamental aspect of prompt engineering is its iterative nature. Project managers 
continuously refine their prompts based on feedback and observations of the LLM’s 
behavior, striving to achieve repeatable, specific outcomes. This iterative approach 
mirrors the agile approaches often employed in project management, emphasizing 
adaptability and continuous improvement.
Prompt engineering empowers project managers to tailor LLM-generated responses 
to complement existing project workflows. Whether it’s generating initial drafts 
of project documents, conducting analyses, or facilitating communication with 
stakeholders, well-crafted prompts can significantly enhance the efficiency and 
effectiveness of AI-powered tools. 
It’s important to recognize that while generative AI can be a powerful ally in project 
management, it isn’t without its limitations. Project professionals play a crucial role in 
overseeing AI-generated outputs, ensuring they align with project requirements and 
objectives. While AI can automate certain tasks, it shouldn’t replace human oversight 
entirely, especially in areas where accuracy and completeness are paramount.

Checklist: Effective Prompt Characteristics
This checklist outlines key characteristics essential for crafting effective prompts, ensuring clarity, 
relevance, and reliability in AI responses.
Specificity
Is the prompt specific and clear in its instructions?
Context
Does the prompt provide enough relevant 
information for the AI to understand the task at 
hand without overwhelming it with unnecessary 
details?
Role
Does the prompt provide a role or character that 
will help the AI system identify the most relevant 
data from its training to apply to your task?
Tone
Is the tone of the prompt appropriate for the 
desired output, whether it’s professional, casual, 
friendly, and so on?
Examples
Are examples included to help the AI better 
understand the prompt and align its responses 
with expectations?
Experimentation and Refinement
Have you experimented with different prompts and 
refined them based on the AI’s responses and evolving 
goals?
Audience Tailoring
Is the prompt tailored to the audience’s understanding 
and needs, including your own understanding if you’re 
the audience?
Clarity
Is the prompt free from jargon, acronyms, and 
ambiguous language to prevent misunderstandings?
Structure
Is the tone of the prompt appropriate for the desired 
output, whether it’s professional, casual, friendly, and 
so on?
Reliability Check
Have you incorporated a reliability check by asking 
for references or sources to ensure the generated 
content meets accuracy and reliability standards?

Validation check
Ensure prompts include a validation check to mitigate “LLM hallucination,” where the LLM generates 
factually incorrect information. Request citations and spot-checking against known facts.
Augment data
Augment LLM data with reliable sources to address noncurrent or incomplete training data, ensuring

Flipped Interaction
The flipped-interaction pattern reverses usual roles 
to help a user get the response they’re seeking when 
they might not know what to prompt the LLM. Instead 
of responding to a question or performing a task, the 
LLM is prompted to proactively question the user 
to draw out more detailed information. The system 
does this until it’s confident enough to provide a 
relevant result that’s customized to the user’s specific 
context and needs. 
This pattern is best when you don’t know all the steps 
needed for a task/project and is most effective 
for gathering detailed project requirements and 
specifications from stakeholders, ensuring project 
alignment.

The concept of prompt chaining involves breaking down complex tasks into smaller subtasks, 
allowing for more efficient management of requests made to language models like LLMs. 
By structuring prompts to focus on one or two tasks at a time, you can enhance the AI’s 
comprehension and output quality. For instance, a prompt initially requesting several tasks 
may result in incomplete or unsatisfactory responses. Refining the prompt using the CREATE 
formula, which involves specifying tasks step by step, can improve results
How to Prompt Chain
When refining prompts, it’s essential to document each prompt for future reference and 
improvement. A prompt refinement template can streamline this process, aiding in the 
identification of effective strategies and facilitating knowledge sharing among team members. 
This template typically includes sections for detailing the task, patterns used, LLM model 
and version, prompt techniques (such as prompt chaining), and metrics for evaluating output 
quality. Regular review and updating of this documentation are necessary to adapt to 
changes in LLM performance and to identify trends and best practices. Feel free to use and
