b. Expect Roles to Blur
The collaboration is even closer than in
traditional products, e.g.:
PM reviews traces with data
scientist and domain experts.
Product designer experiments with
vibe-engineering tools.
Engineers deepen domain expertise
to interpret outputs and suggest
solutions.
     The key trait is curiosity. Also, screen  
     hard for: data intuition, product sense, 
     and urgency.
c. Test for AI Thinking
Skip theory trivia. Use hands-on
exercises. For example:
Give a small, messy slice from evals
or user logs. 
Ask to review traces, identify failure
modes, and propose evaluators.
Ask to design the smallest agent.
Discuss observability, guardrails,
evals, etc.
     What matters is how candidates
     reason, collaborate, use judgement and 
     adapt in ambiguous problem spaces.
