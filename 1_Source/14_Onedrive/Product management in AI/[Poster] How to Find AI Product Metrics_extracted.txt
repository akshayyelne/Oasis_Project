Generic AI Metrics Don’t Work
Generic metrics, such as “hallucination” or “toxicity”
often miss domain-specific issues. Their abuse is
endemic in the industry. The most successful teams
look at data, identify failure modes, and let app-specific
metrics emerge bottom-up.

It’s critical to align LLM-as-Judge evals with human experts. Measure:
