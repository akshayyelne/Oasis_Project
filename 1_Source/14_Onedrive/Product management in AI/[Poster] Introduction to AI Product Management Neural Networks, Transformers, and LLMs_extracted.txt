Tokenization: The input data (like text) is split into tokens.
Positional encoding: Encoding a position to each token so
the model can understand, e.g., the order of words.
Self-attention: Used by encoders and decoders. It allows
each node to interact with all other nodes.
Feedforward network: Passes the data to the next layer.
Masked attention: Prevents future tokens from being seen
during generation.

Basic Terms
Supervised Learning: Model is trained on a labeled dataset
(input + correct output). This is the basis for fine-tuning.
Unsupervised  Learning: Model  learns  from  data  without
labels, discovering patterns and structures independently.
Reinforcement Learning: Model interacts with an environment,
receiving rewards or penalties to learn (e.g., AlphaGo).
Deep Learning: Using neural networks with many layers. Can be
used in supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning.
Neural  Networks:  Computational  models  inspired  by  the
human brain, consisting of layers of interconnected nodes
("neurons") that process data.
Transformers: Architecture for sequential data, using self-
attention to weigh the importance of different input parts.
Large Language Models (LLMs): Trained on large datasets
to  generate  and  understand  human-like  text.  Combine
supervised, unsupervised, and deep learning.
