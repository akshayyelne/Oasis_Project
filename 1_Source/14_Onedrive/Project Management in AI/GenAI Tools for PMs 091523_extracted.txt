Generative AI (GenAI) offers project practitioners significant support during the project planning 
phase by automating tasks, analyzing historical data, and providing valuable insights. Through 
its ability to generate preliminary project plans, optimize resource allocation, assess risks, and 
predict outcomes, GenAI streamlines the planning process and enhances decision-making. 

GenAI supports efficiencies in time and cost management by automating calculations, optimizing 
schedules, and predicting risks. It ensures better estimations, cost savings, and on-time project 
delivery through data analysis and forecasting capabilities.
