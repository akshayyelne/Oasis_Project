## What We Discovered

After reviewing the most comprehensive Claude Code project structure guides, I've put together something I wish I had when starting: **a complete audit framework you can use right now**.

It covers:

### **The Three Pillars of Good Project Structure**

1. **CLAUDE.md** — Your project's operating manual (< 300 lines is the sweet spot)
2. **.claude/ folder** — The control center where commands, skills, and hooks live
3. **Directory organization** — Everything related to your project in one place

### **The Audit Framework** 
A practical scorecard that evaluates your project across 6 dimensions:
- Documentation quality (is CLAUDE.md there and clear?)
- Directory structure (can a newcomer find what they need?)
- .claude/ setup (are your controls organized?)
- Code organization standards (consistent patterns?)
- Context management (is the AI getting what it needs?)
- Team scalability (could others maintain this?)

### **3 Ways to Audit Your Project**

**Option A (15 minutes):** Quick self-assessment checklist  
**Option B (2-3 hours):** Deep dive with bash commands that analyze your repo
